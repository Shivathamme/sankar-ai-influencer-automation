# Sample message examples for the assignment

These are illustrative templates. The live app asks the selected AI provider to personalise each message from verified profile and campaign facts. Demo-mode output is synthetic.

## Initial outreach

**Subject:** Potential creator collaboration

Hello [First name],

I’m reaching out from [Brand]. We are exploring a collaboration with creators whose publicly available profile information aligns with our [campaign niche] campaign. Your profile is listed in the [niche] category, which appears relevant to the audience we hope to reach. We would be glad to share the campaign brief, proposed deliverables and compensation details for your consideration.

Would you be open to receiving more information?

[Sender identity, postal address and opt-out wording are appended by the application.]

## Follow-up 1

**Subject:** Re: Potential creator collaboration

Hello [First name],

I wanted to follow up on my earlier note about a possible partnership with [Brand]. If the campaign is relevant to your content and audience, we can share the brief and discuss the deliverables and proposed terms. If it is not a fit, no action is needed.

Would you like me to send the details?

## Follow-up 2

**Subject:** Re: Potential creator collaboration

Hello [First name],

This is my final follow-up about the potential [Brand] collaboration. If you would like to explore it, simply reply and we can share the campaign details. Otherwise, I will close the loop and will not send further follow-ups.

Thank you for your time.

## Example reply classification

**Incoming:** “Thanks for reaching out. What is the budget for one reel?”

**Classification:** `pricing_enquiry`

**Suggested next action:** Have the partnerships team confirm the budget and deliverables.

**Draft reply:** “Thanks for your reply. Our team will confirm the proposed budget and deliverables shortly.”

All examples must be reviewed and edited for the real brand, recipient and campaign before sending.
